# Terms of use

Read this before you run the kit or act on one of its reports.

## What the report is

- **It is an automated audit.** A program read your site and wrote the report. It can miss problems,
  and it can flag things that are not problems.
- **No guarantee.** Nothing in the report promises better rankings, more traffic, more leads or more
  revenue.
- **Some advice may not apply to you.** A finding can be correct in general and still be wrong for
  your business, your site or your situation. Check with your web person before you change anything.
- **You decide.** You choose which changes to make. The template creator and the bot that ran the
  audit are not responsible for any loss of traffic, rankings, leads or revenue that comes from
  following, or not following, the report.

## What the kit does to your site

- **Nothing is changed on your site.** The crawl is read-only: it sends only GET and HEAD requests.
  It never logs in, fills in a form, posts data or edits anything.
- **It is polite.** By default it reads at most 500 pages, 2 requests at a time, 1 second apart,
  obeys robots.txt (and its Crawl-delay), and identifies itself as
  `GrokBot-SiteAudit (+https://github.com/PensacolaSeoCompany/site-audit-kit)`.

## Consent

Crawl a site only if you own or manage it, or its owner gave you permission. A bot that uses this kit
must ask "Do you own or manage this site, or have permission to audit it?" and wait for a yes before
each crawl.

## Your data

The crawl data and the reports stay on the computer that ran the audit. They are not sent to the
template creator or to anyone else. You choose whether to share a report.
